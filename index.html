Sources and licences資料來源與授權

  • Boundaries: Derived from MOI district boundaries (2025-03-18) and NLSC village boundaries (2026-08-17) via plotdb/pdmaptw v2.4.0; Open Government Data License v1.0
  • Boundaries are simplified: use them for context, not for measurement or precise overlay. The old-downtown and Xitun–Beitun files are subsets of the former-city files, with identical geometry and attributes. Futai Village (富台里, East District) has no boundary in the source; the map shows it as a circle of the same area at its label point.
  • Land-use areas: Urban Development Bureau, Taichung City Government: public briefing for the 2nd comprehensive review of the Shuinan Airport site detailed plan, 2024-02-01
  • Major projects: Taichung City Government project reports and news coverage; each row's source is in the CSV.
  • Detailed plan overlay: colour patches auto-traced from Figure 7-1 (unofficial English reference) and fitted to OpenStreetMap road centrelines; block edges sit a median 11 m from the nearest road, so expect errors of a few tens of metres. The Chinese plan and official GIS/CAD data prevail.
  • Basemap: vector tiles from OpenFreeMap (© OpenMapTiles), drawn with MapLibre GL JS, labelled with English names (OSM name:en) where mapped; the standard OpenStreetMap tiles serve as a fallback under the OSM tile usage policy.
  • © OpenStreetMap contributors: basemap data and the road network (Open Database License 1.0, fetched through the Overpass API).
